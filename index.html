About

CONTROL & EFFICIENCY

Founded in 2020 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, Control & Efficiency is a trusted provider of evaporative air-cooling solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. We offer a wide range of products including portable desert coolers, portable air coolers, and outdoor air conditioners engineered to perform efficiently in the most demanding climates.

VISION

To be Saudi Arabia’s leading outdoor cooling solutions provider, setting new standards in efficiency, innovation, and sustainability.

MISSION

To design and deliver reliable, high-quality outdoor cooling systems that provide comfort, durability, and energy saving for every outdoor environment – from homes and gardens to commercial and industrial spaces.

Engineering Comfort

State-of-the-art manufacturing and rigorous testing processes.
